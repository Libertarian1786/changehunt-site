What ChangeHunt is

ChangeHunt identifies coins and pocket change from a photo and shows a rough value range. It is a research tool for your own curiosity and sorting decisions, not an appraisal service.

Estimates are estimates

ChangeHunt does not appraise anything. Every identification is a best-effort guess from an automated vision model, shown with a confidence level (High, Medium or Low) so you know how much to trust it. A low-confidence result shows up to three possible candidates and a manual search option — it never presents a precise identification it is not confident in.

Every value shown is a range, never a single number, built from curated reference bands or from active marketplace listings (asking prices, not completed transactions), and is clearly labeled as an estimate. Most pocket change is worth its face value; occasional coins are worth more, and the app says so honestly either way.

ChangeHunt is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by PCGS, NGC, or any other coin grading service. Grade bands shown in the app (for example "circulated" or "lightly circulated") are broad, plain-language wear descriptions, not numeric or certified grades, and are not a substitute for professional grading.

We are not liable for losses from decisions you make using the app, including a coin identified incorrectly or a value range that does not match what a coin is later worth to a buyer. For any decision with real money at stake, consult a professional numismatist or grading service.
